Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers the PokemonDex Android app and the website at https://pokemon.laserraptorai.duckdns.org (together, “the service”). The app is a thin wrapper around the website; the same policy applies to both.

What we collect

  • Account data. If you create an account, we store your email address and a password hash. You can use the catalogue without an account.
  • Collection data. If you save cards to a collection, we store those card identifiers and quantities against your account.
  • Technical data. Our web server records standard request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested URL) for security and abuse prevention. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Local storage. The app and website use a session cookie and browser local storage to keep you signed in and remember display preferences.

How we use it

  • To provide the catalogue, search, pricing display and collection features.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To diagnose errors and prevent abuse.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with the infrastructure providers that host the service (server hosting and database) and only as needed to run it. Card data and prices are sourced from public card databases and marketplaces; those sources do not receive your personal data from us.

Retention and deletion

Account and collection data are kept while your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing the address below. Server logs are kept for a short period for security purposes and then discarded.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date at the top shows the current version.
