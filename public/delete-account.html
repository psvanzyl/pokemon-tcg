Delete your account

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your PokemonDex account and the data that belongs to it. It applies to the PokemonDex Android app and the website at https://pokemon.laserraptorai.duckdns.org.

What you can delete

  • Your account: email address and password hash.
  • Your saved collection: card identifiers and quantities linked to your account.

You do not need an account to browse the catalogue. Server request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested URL) are kept for a short period for security and are not linked to your account after deletion.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to psvanzyl@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my PokemonDex account.
  3. Send the email from the address that your account uses. This proves that you own the account.
  4. State in the email if you want the account deleted, the collection deleted, or both.

What happens next

  • We confirm the request within 7 days.
  • The account, the password hash and the saved collection are deleted within 30 days of the request.
  • You receive a short email when the deletion is complete.

Questions

Write to psvanzyl@gmail.com. See also the privacy policy.
